# License status — PROVISIONAL, not ratified

The Apache-2.0 text in `LICENSE` is a **placeholder**, not a decision.

It was written into this repo during bootstrap on the assumption that
busy-office-output's license was family precedent. That was chat-layer
banking of exactly the kind Rule Zero forbids (see busy-office-erp
`CLAUDE.md`): no round-table ratified it, and no sponsor signed it.

The real question is open as **OQ-LICENSE-ENGINE** in
busy-office-erp/DESIGN-GRAPH.md, owner: sponsor. It turns on whether the
long-term business is the ERP product or the methodology:

- **Apache-2.0** — category play; keeps tier-B keyless attestation free
  for public repos.
- **BSL with a change date** — protects the methodology itself.

Until that question closes, treat this repo's license as **undecided**.
Do not rely on the Apache-2.0 grant, and do not distribute this code
externally on the strength of it.
